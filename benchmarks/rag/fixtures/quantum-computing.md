# Quantum Computing Notes

## Entanglement

Quantum entanglement lets two particles share correlated states: measuring one
instantly determines the other, regardless of distance.

## Superposition

A qubit in superposition represents both zero and one simultaneously until
measured. This parallelism underpins quantum speedups.

## Error Correction

Practical quantum computers require error correction codes that spread a
logical qubit across many physical qubits.
